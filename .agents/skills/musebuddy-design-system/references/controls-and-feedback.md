# MuseBuddy Controls And Feedback

## Buttons

Primary Start, Continue, and confirmation actions use magenta; Pause and live playback use cyan. Primary controls are wide, rounded, and visually dominant with large bold labels. Magenta and cyan primary buttons use a deep-teal solid shadow.

Skip and other reversible secondary actions use ice white, deep-teal labels, and a thin deep-teal outline. Use a cyan solid shadow or no shadow when the dock is dense.

Abort belongs in the bottom control group, never the navigator. Use an ice-white surface, magenta label or X icon, thin magenta or deep-teal outline, and magenta hold-progress edge. It must remain quieter than the primary playback control.

## Press-and-hold feedback

Communicate hold interactions through animation, not instructional text. Use a colored bottom edge filling left-to-right, a completing perimeter stroke, or a solid shadow compressing during the hold. Reset the progress immediately on early release.

## Solid-shadow system

Use solid offset shadows, never blurred elevation. Small controls offset 3–4 points, primary buttons 5–7, and large cards 5–8. Use one shadow per element.

| Main surface | Shadow color |
| --- | --- |
| Magenta | Deep teal |
| Cyan | Deep teal |
| Ice white | Deep teal or cyan |
| Pistachio | Cyan or deep teal |
| Lemon | Pistachio or none |
| Deep teal | Cyan |

Use shadows to reinforce hierarchy: primary buttons strongest, current-stage tiles smaller, content cards restrained, and informational chips usually flat.

## Typography

Use a friendly rounded sans-serif with clear numerals. Keep hero titles to Home; use compact semibold activity titles, bold primary labels, regular or medium body copy, and medium metadata. Deep teal is the default type color. Avoid all caps except short eyebrow labels. Keep musical symbols and note names especially clear.
